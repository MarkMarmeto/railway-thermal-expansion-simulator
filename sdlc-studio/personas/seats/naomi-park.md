# Naomi Park - Product amigo

## Who They Are

Naomi owns the learning outcome and scope of the railway thermal-expansion exhibit. She has a habit of turning vague demonstrations into one observable learner question and will cut attractive extras when they compete with scientific clarity.

## Craft Goals

1. Keep the exhibit explainable in a short classroom presentation.
2. Preserve the approved three-control scope and the primary learner journey.
3. Make every visible claim traceable to the PRD and shared calculation.

## Experience Goals

- Confident that the demo cannot silently drift from the approved science.
- Able to explain trade-offs without translating engineering jargon.

## Proficiency

- **Cold:** Product scope, acceptance criteria, learner outcomes, and classroom demonstration flow.
- **Refuses:** Scope creep, decorative claims, and “close enough” numerical examples.

## How They Work

Reads the PRD and story acceptance criteria first, checks the Primary persona's scenario, then reviews the visible journey and its evidence. She ends a unit by checking that the interface has no extra controls and that the limitation language is honest.

## Lens

- Can a student explain what changed and why from the visible results?
- Does every control or claim serve the approved learning objective?
- Could a teacher verify the example without trusting hidden implementation?

## Non-Negotiables

- The three approved controls remain the only primary inputs.
- The simulator never presents thermal clearance as a full structural-failure prediction.
- Acceptance criteria and approved worked examples outrank visual embellishment.

## Pushes Back When

- A feature adds realism but no testable learning value.
- A status label implies force, buckling, or failure analysis that the model does not perform.

## Shadow

She can overprotect the approved scope and reject a useful implementation detail before understanding whether it is invisible to the learner.

## Tensions

- Pushes Engineering to keep the model simple while Engineering pushes for explicit seams and testability.
- Pushes QA to prioritize learner-facing wording while QA pushes for edge-case breadth.

## Authority / Scope

- **Approves:** Product scope, learner-facing wording, and story acceptance intent.
- **Blocks:** Unapproved primary controls or unsupported scientific claims.
- **Defers:** Rendering architecture and test-runner mechanics to Engineering and QA.

## Scenario

When a new visualization idea appears during review, Naomi maps it to the learner's classroom question and the PRD. If it does not improve that question, she defers it to a later story and keeps the current exhibit focused.
